## In his own words
**What do you do, and what are you great at?**
I'm great at leading a team and creating something from nothing — give me an empty timeline
and an empty IDE, or loose requirements, and I can get you where you wanted to go.

**Why AI and platform product work? What pulled you from engineering into PM?**
I loved creating things as a developer but realized my skillset was better suited to
ideating, leading, and finding the right solutions. Product and development is an economy of
ideas — my job is to take all of them in and pick the best ones. Those ideas don't have to
come from me, as long as I give credit where it's due. I don't need the glory; that's what
makes a good leader.

**How do you work with engineers?**
I take their input seriously — they're the experts in their domain, and I'm the expert in
mine and in the business objectives. When everyone stays in their lane and frees each other
up to do their best work, that's when the team does its best work.

**How do you use AI day to day?**
Daily — to ideate, to develop, and to stay organized.

**A failure or lesson learned?**
Iterating is the best option. Too often we try to bite the whole elephant in one bite. The
best way to tackle almost any problem is one bite at a time.

**What kind of role are you looking for next?**
A role that includes AI integrations, leading a team to move quickly and efficiently on
solutions that actually help people.

**What does touring stage management have in common with product management?**
Everything — it's all ego management. On tour, the show must go on, and trade-offs happen in
snap decisions constantly. Product ownership is the same: it's yours, and you often have to
make concessions to keep the show going. The trick is getting good at making the right
trade-offs at the right times — I've been training for that my whole career.

**Personal touches**
Being the child of immigrants (Cuban-American) taught me to be scrappy and make things work,
and that loyalty, honor, and respect are indispensable. Character is something you never
compromise on.
